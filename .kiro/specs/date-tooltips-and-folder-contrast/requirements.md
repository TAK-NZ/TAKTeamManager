# Requirements Document

## Introduction

This spec covers TWO unrelated presentational corrections. They share a document because they were raised together and both are small, and for no other reason: they touch disjoint code, fail in disjoint ways, and either can ship without the other. They are kept in separate requirements throughout so that neither can be reviewed, implemented, or reverted on the strength of the other.

**Concern A — hover context on every rendered date and time (Requirements 1–4).** Every user-visible date in the application already goes through the two shared Date_Format_Helpers in `client/src/utils/dateFormat.js`, which render `yyyy-mm-dd` and `yyyy-mm-dd HH:MM` in a configurable Display_Timezone (device-management Requirement 18). What a rendered date does not currently carry is the two facts that make it readable at a glance: which zone it is presented in, and how long ago — or how far ahead — it falls. Concern A adds both as a Date_Tooltip disclosed on hover AND on keyboard focus. **Like the Display_Timezone before it, this reaches app-wide and that is the chosen scope, not an oversight — see the scope flag in Requirement 2.** Nothing about the rendered date strings themselves changes.

**Concern B — the channel-tree folder icon fails contrast in dark mode (Requirements 5–7).** Measured, not reported: the folder icon in the channel tree renders `text-blue-600` (#2563eb) on a dark row background of `bg-gray-600` (#4b5563), a contrast ratio of **1.46:1** against the 3:1 minimum WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.11 sets for graphical objects. This is a conformance failure, not a legibility preference. It is confined to the two Channel_Tree_Pages and reaches nothing else in the application.

### Measurement corrections

Three figures were reported for Concern B and are corrected here, because two of them change what the fix has to be. Every ratio in this document is computed from the Tailwind palette hex values with the WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance formula (Requirement 7 makes the computation a test rather than a footnote).

1. **The reported ~1.9:1 is not the folder row.** `text-blue-600` on `bg-gray-600` is **1.46:1**. The 1.99:1 figure belongs to `text-blue-600` on `bg-gray-700` — the *adjacent* Expandable_Channel_Row. Both row types fail; the folder row is the worse of the two. This is independent support for the unification Requirement 6 asks for.
2. **Moving the dark background to `gray-800` alone does NOT clear the threshold.** `text-blue-600` on `bg-gray-800` (#1f2937) is **2.84:1**, still below 3:1. The reported 3.4:1 corresponds to `gray-900` (#111827), not `gray-800`. The consequence is material: the icon change to `dark:text-blue-400` is **load-bearing, not headroom**. See Criteria 5.4 and 5.5.
3. `dark:text-blue-400` (#60a5fa) on `bg-gray-800` is **5.77:1**, close to the reported ~5.9 and comfortably clear.

## Glossary

- **Date_Format_Helpers**: The shared `formatDate(value, fallback)` and `formatDateTime(value, fallback)` functions in `client/src/utils/dateFormat.js` — the single place the application turns an instant into the `yyyy-mm-dd` / `yyyy-mm-dd HH:MM` string it shows a user. Established by device-management Requirement 18, which also gives them the Display_Timezone.
- **Display_Timezone**: The IANA zone every user-visible date is rendered in, configured via `DISPLAY_TIMEZONE`, defaulting to `Pacific/Auckland`. Presentation only. Specified by device-management Requirement 18 and NOT re-specified here.
- **Resolved_Display_Timezone**: The zone actually in force — the value `getDisplayTimezone()` returns, i.e. the zone AFTER the Display_Timezone_Fallback_Chain has resolved. It is deliberately the resolved zone and never the configured one, so a mistyped `DISPLAY_TIMEZONE` reports the zone dates are really rendered in rather than the one that was asked for (device-management Criterion 18.8). This function already exists and needs no new plumbing; it is the whole of the timezone half of Concern A.
- **Date_Render_Position**: One place in the client that renders a Date_Format_Helpers return value into the document. There are TEN, in six files (the reported figure of eight undercounts): `components/DeviceListRow.jsx` Issued, Expires and Last Seen; `pages/AuditLogs.jsx` timestamp; `pages/Users.jsx` last login; `pages/Requests.jsx` two "Submitted" values; `pages/Admin.jsx` last sync and template last-updated; `components/OrgInterestRequests.jsx` created-at. The Last Seen cell reaches `formatDateTime` from two branches, so there are ELEVEN call sites behind those ten positions.
- **Formatted_Date**: The ONE shared client component, at `client/src/components/FormattedDate.jsx`, through which every Date_Render_Position renders its value and acquires its Date_Tooltip. It wraps the Date_Format_Helpers rather than replacing them: the helpers keep producing the string, and this component adds the disclosure around it. See Requirement 2.
- **Date_Tooltip**: The disclosure a Formatted_Date reveals on pointer hover AND on keyboard focus, carrying exactly two facts: the Relative_Time phrase and the Resolved_Display_Timezone. See Requirements 2 and 3.
- **Relative_Time**: The English phrase describing the signed distance between a rendered instant and the moment the Date_Tooltip is disclosed — `just now`, `3 minutes ago`, `in 2 days`. Produced by a pure, total classifier in `client/src/utils/relativeTime.js`, following the `client/src/utils/expiryWarning.js` convention of extracting the decidable logic out of the component that renders it. See Requirement 1.
- **Relative_Time_Ladder**: The six ordered Relative_Time_Buckets of Criterion 1.3, evaluated on the absolute distance, with the direction supplied separately. Exactly one bucket matches any finite distance.
- **Relative_Time_Bucket**: One rung of the Relative_Time_Ladder: a half-open distance range, a unit, and the two phrase templates (past and future) for that unit.
- **Nominal_Month**: Exactly 30 days (2 592 000 000 ms). **Nominal_Year**: Exactly 365 days (31 536 000 000 ms). Both are deliberate approximations — see Criterion 1.5.
- **No_Phrase**: The explicit result the Relative_Time classifier returns for input it cannot place — null, undefined, a non-date, an unparseable string, `NaN`, `Infinity`. It is a distinct return value, not a thrown error and not a phrase. A Date_Render_Position whose value classifies as No_Phrase renders no Date_Tooltip at all. See Criteria 1.7, 2.7.
- **Disclosure_Time_Computation**: The constraint that a Relative_Time phrase is computed from a clock read at the moment the Date_Tooltip is disclosed, never at the moment the surrounding row renders. See Criteria 2.5, 2.6.
- **Hover_And_Focus_Disclosure**: The existing convention in this codebase that a tooltip appears on pointer hover AND on keyboard focus, established by `components/DeviceTypeIcon.jsx` and `components/DeviceListRow.jsx` (device-management Criteria 16.3, 16.4) with `group-hover:opacity-100 group-focus-within:opacity-100` plus a focusable host.
- **Sideways_Tooltip_Placement**: The tooltip placement this codebase arrived at after a measured clipping defect: anchored to the host's left or right edge (`left-full` / `right-full`) and vertically centred on its row (`top-1/2 -translate-y-1/2`), never above or below it. See Tooltip_Clipping_Defect and Criterion 3.4.
- **Tooltip_Clipping_Defect**: The observed defect that produced Sideways_Tooltip_Placement. Device type icon tooltips were reported "cut off, behind a frame". The cause is recorded in `DeviceTypeIcon.jsx`: both device tables carry `overflow-x-auto`, and a box with one overflow axis `auto` and the other `visible` clips on BOTH axes, so an upward-opening tooltip on the first row is clipped by the wrapper's top edge and a horizontally-centred one on a leading column is clipped — and left unreachable — past its left edge.
- **Midnight_Anchor**: The instant a date-only rendered value is compared from and to for Relative_Time purposes: 00:00 in the Resolved_Display_Timezone on the calendar day the cell displays, with `now` anchored the same way. See Requirement 4.
- **Contrast_Ratio**: The WCAG 2.1 ratio `(L_lighter + 0.05) / (L_darker + 0.05)`, where L is relative luminance computed from the sRGB colour values.
- **Graphical_Object_Minimum**: 3:1 — the Contrast_Ratio WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.11 (Non-text Contrast) requires of graphical objects and user-interface components. The Folder_Icon and the Disclosure_Chevron are both graphical objects under this criterion.
- **Text_Contrast_Minimum**: 4.5:1 — the Contrast_Ratio WCAG 2.1 SC 1.4.3 requires of normal-size body text, applicable to the row text that shares a Channel_Tree_Row's background.
- **Channel_Tree_Pages**: The two files that render the channel folder tree, `client/src/pages/Dashboard.jsx` and `client/src/pages/GlobalChannels.jsx`. Both contain the same folder-row markup, independently duplicated.
- **Folder_Row**: The clickable folder row in a channel tree — today `className="flex items-center p-3 bg-gray-100 dark:bg-gray-600 rounded-lg cursor-pointer hover:bg-gray-200 dark:hover:bg-gray-500"`, at `Dashboard.jsx` ~327–330 and `GlobalChannels.jsx` ~211–214.
- **Expandable_Channel_Row**: The adjacent row type in `Dashboard.jsx` (~262) that renders a channel which is also a folder, today `bg-gray-50 dark:bg-gray-700 ... ml-6`. It carries no row-level hover of its own; its toggle button carries `hover:bg-gray-200 dark:hover:bg-gray-600`.
- **Channel_Tree_Row**: A Folder_Row or an Expandable_Channel_Row. Requirement 6 gives the two one background.
- **Folder_Icon**: The `FolderIcon` / `FolderOpenIcon` heroicon inside a Folder_Row, today `className="h-5 w-5 text-blue-600 mr-2"`. Both glyphs carry identical colour classes and both are in scope wherever this document says Folder_Icon.
- **Disclosure_Chevron**: The `ChevronRightIcon` / `ChevronRightSmall` in a Channel_Tree_Row, today `text-gray-500`, whose rotation is the row's only expanded/collapsed affordance. In scope for contrast only, and flagged in Criterion 5.7 as an addition beyond what was reported.
- **Color_Token_Table**: The single mapping from each Tailwind colour token in use by the Channel_Tree_Rows to its sRGB hex value, held in one place so the Requirement 7 assertions compute from real colours. Its values are derived from this project's own declared palette in `client/tailwind.config.js` — which defines `theme.extend.colors.gray` 50–900 and a custom `primary` scale — rather than from hex literals or from stock Tailwind. The declared `gray` scale happens to match stock Tailwind today, which is exactly why the table sources from the config instead of restating it. See Criterion 7.3.

## Requirements

### Requirement 1: The Relative_Time Ladder

**User Story:** As a user reading a date in a list, I want to know how long ago it was without doing the arithmetic myself, so that a timestamp tells me something at a glance.

The classifier is a pure function of two instants — the rendered value and a supplied `now` — with `now` as a parameter rather than an internal `Date.now()` read, so every boundary in this requirement is testable by arithmetic. This is the same shape `client/src/utils/expiryWarning.js`'s `classifyExpiry(expiresAt, warningDays, now)` already has, and the reason is the same one recorded there.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Relative_Time classifier SHALL be a pure, total function of a candidate instant and a supplied `now`, taking `now` as a parameter and SHALL NOT read the clock internally.
2. THE Relative_Time classifier SHALL derive the direction from the sign of `value − now` alone: a value strictly earlier than `now` is past and renders the `... ago` template; a value strictly later renders the `in ...` template. A value equal to `now` falls in the first Relative_Time_Bucket, whose phrase is directionless.
3. THE Relative_Time classifier SHALL place the absolute distance `|value − now|` into exactly one Relative_Time_Bucket, evaluating these half-open ranges and stopping at the first match:
   1. distance < 1 minute → `just now`, with no direction and no magnitude.
   2. 1 minute ≤ distance < 1 hour → `<n> minutes ago` / `in <n> minutes`, n = floor(distance / 1 minute).
   3. 1 hour ≤ distance < 1 day → `<n> hours ago` / `in <n> hours`, n = floor(distance / 1 hour).
   4. 1 day ≤ distance < 1 Nominal_Month → `<n> days ago` / `in <n> days`, n = floor(distance / 1 day).
   5. 1 Nominal_Month ≤ distance < 1 Nominal_Year → `<n> months ago` / `in <n> months`, n = floor(distance / 1 Nominal_Month).
   6. 1 Nominal_Year ≤ distance → `<n> years ago` / `in <n> years`, n = floor(distance / 1 Nominal_Year).
4. THE Relative_Time classifier SHALL render the sub-minute bucket as `just now` and SHALL NOT render it as `0 minutes ago`, `in 0 minutes`, or any other zero-magnitude phrase. A magnitude of zero is never emitted: every bucket from the second onward has a lower bound of exactly one of its own units, so its floored magnitude is at least 1.
5. THE Relative_Time classifier SHALL treat a month as exactly 30 days and a year as exactly 365 days. **This is a deliberate approximation and the resulting drift is accepted, and is recorded here as a decision open to review.** Calendar months are uneven and years are not all 365 days; correct calendar arithmetic would mean carrying month-length and leap-year handling for a tooltip, and a phrase that is already rounded to whole units gains nothing measurable from it. Two visible artifacts follow and are accepted rather than papered over: a value 31 days old reads `1 month ago` when the calendar month it spans may be longer; and because 365 / 30 = 12.17, the month bucket's top magnitude is **12**, not 11, so `12 months ago` is reachable immediately below the one-year boundary.
6. THE Relative_Time classifier SHALL FLOOR every magnitude, so a distance of 1 hour 59 minutes reads `1 hour ago`. **Rounding to nearest was considered and rejected, and this too is a decision open to review.** Flooring is the conventional behaviour, and more importantly it never claims more elapsed time than has elapsed — which matters because the phrase sits directly beside the absolute date the user can check. Rounding to nearest would say `2 hours ago` about a value 1 hour 31 minutes old, so the phrase and the visible timestamp would disagree by an hour for a user who did the subtraction.
7. THE Relative_Time classifier SHALL singularise at a magnitude of exactly 1, emitting `1 minute ago`, `in 1 minute`, `1 hour ago`, `1 day ago`, `1 month ago`, `1 year ago`, and SHALL NOT emit `1 minutes ago` or any other plural at magnitude 1. Every magnitude of 2 or more SHALL be plural.
8. THE Relative_Time classifier SHALL be total and SHALL NOT throw for any input. IF the candidate instant is null, undefined, a non-string non-number non-Date value, an unparseable string, `NaN`, or non-finite, THEN THE Relative_Time classifier SHALL return No_Phrase. IF the supplied `now` is itself unusable in any of those ways, THEN THE Relative_Time classifier SHALL return No_Phrase.
9. THE Relative_Time classifier SHALL return No_Phrase as a distinct value rather than an empty string, so that a caller can tell "there is nothing to say about this value" apart from "the phrase happened to be empty", and so that Criterion 2.7's suppression of the whole tooltip is driven by a value rather than by a string test.
10. THE Relative_Time classifier SHALL assemble every phrase from English string literals held in this repository and SHALL NOT use `Intl.RelativeTimeFormat` or any other locale-aware formatter. The reason is the one device-management Criterion 18.3 already records for the Date_Format_Helpers: a locale-dependent rendering would make the phrase vary with the browser's locale while the date beside it does not, and this application standardises on locale-independent presentation. It would also not produce the fixed Relative_Time_Ladder or the Nominal_Month/Nominal_Year units this requirement specifies.
11. THE Relative_Time classifier SHALL be covered by a fast-check property test at `client/src/utils/relativeTime.property.test.js`, following the `client/src/utils/expiryWarning.property.test.js` convention, asserting all four of:
    1. **Totality** — for arbitrary input drawn from a generator that includes strings, numbers, `null`, `undefined`, objects, `NaN` and non-finite values, the classifier returns either a phrase or No_Phrase and never throws.
    2. **Bucket selection** — for every boundary in Criterion 1.3, a distance of exactly the boundary, one millisecond below it, and one millisecond above it each lands in the bucket Criterion 1.3 assigns, in both directions.
    3. **Sign** — a value earlier than `now` never produces an `in ...` phrase and a value later never produces an `... ago` phrase.
    4. **Singularisation** — a magnitude of exactly 1 is singular and a magnitude of 2 or more is plural, in every unit and both directions.
12. THE Relative_Time property test SHALL draw its distances from **boundary-concentrated generators** — each boundary offset by a small jitter, combined via `fc.oneof` with a broad uniform generator — and SHALL NOT rely on the uniform generator alone. A uniform offset over a range of years lands on or adjacent to a boundary with vanishing probability, and the boundaries are precisely where an implementation that wrote `<` for `<=` is wrong; a uniform-only generator would report a clean pass on that bug. This mirrors how device-management Criterion 21.11 handled the expiry boundaries.

### Requirement 2: Every Rendered Date Carries Hover Context, From One Shared Component

**User Story:** As a user, I want hovering any date in the application to tell me the zone it is shown in and how long ago it was, so that I get the same context wherever a date appears.

**Scope note — this requirement deliberately reaches app-wide, and that is the chosen scope, not an oversight.** It changes what every one of the ten Date_Render_Positions renders: the device lists, the audit log, the Users view, both requests lists, both admin surfaces, and the org-interest requests. Pages with nothing to do with either concern in this document WILL gain the disclosure. That is the point — a per-page tooltip would mean two dates on one screen behaving differently — but it is the widest-reaching change in this spec and **it needs flagging in review**, exactly as device-management Requirement 18 flagged the Display_Timezone that preceded it and for the same reason.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL be the ONE place the Date_Tooltip is implemented, and every Date_Render_Position SHALL adopt it. THE Date_Tooltip SHALL NOT be added per page, per table, or per cell.
2. WHEN a new Date_Render_Position is added anywhere in the application, THE Formatted_Date component SHALL be the means by which it renders its value, and it SHALL thereby acquire the Date_Tooltip with no further work. A new date render site SHALL NOT have to opt in to the behaviour.
3. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL render exactly the string the corresponding Date_Format_Helpers call renders today, character for character, and SHALL NOT change any rendered date or time text. This requirement ADDS a disclosure; it does not restyle, reformat, or re-zone a single value.
4. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL preserve each adopting caller's own fallback string unchanged where the caller renders one instead of a date: `'-'` in `OrgInterestRequests.jsx`, `'Never'` in `Users.jsx`, `'Unknown'` for Issued and Expires and `'never seen'` for Last Seen in `DeviceListRow.jsx`, and the raw `row.created_at` value `AuditLogs.jsx` passes as its own fallback.
5. WHEN a Date_Tooltip is disclosed, THE Formatted_Date component SHALL compute its Relative_Time phrase from a clock read at that moment (Disclosure_Time_Computation), and SHALL recompute it on each subsequent disclosure. A phrase computed when the surrounding row rendered would be wrong by the age of the render: a row that said `5 minutes ago` would still say it twenty minutes later.
6. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL satisfy Criterion 2.5 without any timer, interval, or subscription. **A ticking timer was considered and rejected**: it would be a second refresh mechanism beside the one device-management Requirement 19 already installed, and it would be per-rendered-date rather than per-view. Note also why the existing refresh does not remove the need for Criterion 2.5 — the Dashboard device card's 60-second re-fetch happens to re-render its dates often enough to mask the staleness on exactly ONE of the ten Date_Render_Positions' surfaces, leaving the audit log, the Users view, both requests lists, both admin surfaces and the org-interest requests wrong on a long-open page.
7. WHERE a Date_Render_Position's value classifies as No_Phrase, THE Formatted_Date component SHALL render NO Date_Tooltip at all — no disclosure host, no `aria-describedby`, no hover or focus affordance — rather than a tooltip carrying an empty phrase, the text `Invalid Date`, or the zone alone. A value the Date_Format_Helpers could not render is a value the tooltip has nothing to say about.
8. THE Date_Tooltip SHALL carry exactly two facts, in this order: the Relative_Time phrase, then the Resolved_Display_Timezone. The phrase leads because it is what the hover was for; the zone qualifies it.
9. THE Date_Tooltip SHALL take its zone from `getDisplayTimezone()` — the Resolved_Display_Timezone — and SHALL NOT read the configured `display_timezone` value directly, so that WHERE `DISPLAY_TIMEZONE` names a zone the runtime rejected, the tooltip names the zone the date is really rendered in rather than the one that was asked for (device-management Criterion 18.8).
10. THE Date_Tooltip SHALL separate its two facts with explicit separator text rather than by adjacency or layout alone, so that assistive technology reading the tooltip as a single string does not run the phrase into the zone name. This is the same lesson `DeviceListRow.jsx` already records for the Connected_Label and the expiry markers, where the separating space is inside the string on purpose.
11. THE Date_Tooltip SHALL NOT include the underlying UTC ISO-8601 instant. Adding it was considered and rejected: it would present a second, differently-zoned rendering of the same moment beside the first, which is the confusion device-management Requirement 18 exists to remove.
12. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL be the only non-test client module that renders a Date_Format_Helpers return value into the document, and THE Date_Tooltip work SHALL be covered by a drift-guard test asserting that the set of non-test client modules importing the Date_Format_Helpers equals an explicitly enumerated allow-list. A new direct consumer SHALL therefore fail that test, forcing a deliberate decision rather than a silent divergence — the mechanical equivalent of device-management Criterion 16.6's one-shared-row-component rule.
13. THE Date_Format_Helpers SHALL keep their current signatures and behaviour, including the `fallback` argument and its empty-string default (device-management Criterion 18.10). Concern A wraps them and SHALL NOT modify them.

### Requirement 3: The Date_Tooltip Is Reachable, Announced, and Not Clipped

**User Story:** As a keyboard or screen-reader user, I want the context on a date to be as available to me as it is to a mouse user, and as a sighted user I want the tooltip fully visible rather than half-hidden behind a table edge.

The accessibility of this disclosure is the substance of it rather than a finish on it, and there is hard-won precedent in this codebase to follow: `DeviceTypeIcon.jsx` and `DeviceListRow.jsx` both carry Hover_And_Focus_Disclosure and Sideways_Tooltip_Placement, the latter as the direct outcome of the Tooltip_Clipping_Defect.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Date_Tooltip SHALL be disclosed on pointer hover AND on keyboard focus (Hover_And_Focus_Disclosure), and SHALL NOT be reachable by hover alone. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL make its disclosure host focusable so that a keyboard user can reach it.
2. THE Date_Tooltip SHALL be associated with its date programmatically, via `aria-describedby` on the element carrying the rendered date pointing at the element carrying the tooltip text, so that assistive technology can reach the context rather than only sighted users seeing a popover.
3. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL NOT rely on the `title` attribute as the means of satisfying Criterion 3.2. A `title` MAY be present in addition, but it SHALL NOT be considered sufficient on its own: screen-reader support for it is inconsistent, and it is not disclosed on keyboard focus at all, which is the whole of Criterion 3.1. This is the same judgement `DeviceTypeIcon.jsx` already records for its own tooltip.
4. THE Date_Tooltip SHALL use Sideways_Tooltip_Placement — anchored to its host's left or right edge with `left-full` or `right-full`, and vertically centred on its row with `top-1/2 -translate-y-1/2`. It SHALL NOT open upward or downward. **This is the fix for a measured defect and SHALL NOT be "tidied" back to a vertically-opening tooltip.** The Tooltip_Clipping_Defect is recorded in `DeviceTypeIcon.jsx`: device type icon tooltips were reported cut off and behind a frame because the enclosing table carries `overflow-x-auto`, and a box with one overflow axis `auto` and the other `visible` clips on BOTH axes. Eight of the ten Date_Render_Positions are table cells inside those same wrappers, so they inherit the same ancestors and the same failure mode.
5. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL choose the side it opens to such that the tooltip stays inside its scrolling ancestor's content box, following the reasoning already recorded in `DeviceListRow.jsx`: its leading Type column opens rightward from `left-full` and its trailing Actions column opens leftward from `right-full`, because a tooltip pushed past a scroll container's left edge is not merely clipped but unreachable by scrolling.
6. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL drive the disclosure from component state — a pointer-enter/pointer-leave and focus/blur transition — rather than from CSS visibility alone. This is a deliberate divergence from the purely CSS `group-hover:opacity-100` tooltips in `DeviceTypeIcon.jsx` and `DeviceListRow.jsx`, and the reason is Criterion 2.5: those tooltips carry STATIC text that cannot go stale, whereas a CSS-only tooltip's content is fixed when the row renders, which is exactly what Disclosure_Time_Computation forbids. The visual treatment SHALL otherwise match the existing tooltips rather than introducing a second look.
7. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL NOT leave an `aria-describedby` pointing at an element that is not in the document. WHILE the Date_Tooltip is not disclosed, either the attribute SHALL be absent or the referenced element SHALL be present.
8. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL keep the two non-table Date_Render_Positions — the last-sync and template-last-updated values in `pages/Admin.jsx`, which render inside `<p>` elements rather than table cells — on the same Sideways_Tooltip_Placement as the other eight, so the application has ONE tooltip behaviour rather than one per surrounding element type.
9. THE Date_Tooltip SHALL NOT convey any information by colour alone, consistent with device-management Criteria 16.5, 20.9 and 21.3. Its content is text.
10. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL NOT change the focus order or the interactive semantics of the row a date sits in beyond adding its own focusable disclosure host, and SHALL NOT make a date cell announce as a button or a link.
11. THE Date_Tooltip SHALL be covered by client tests asserting the focus-visible disclosure, the `aria-describedby` association, the absence of a tooltip for a No_Phrase value, and the Sideways_Tooltip_Placement classes — so that the Tooltip_Clipping_Defect cannot be reintroduced silently, and so an accessible disclosure cannot regress into a hover-only popover. This extends device-management Criterion 16.7's obligation to this component.

### Requirement 4: Date-Only Values Are Anchored at Midnight in the Display Timezone

**User Story:** As a user hovering a date that shows no time of day, I want the phrase to describe the day I can see, so that the tooltip and the cell agree.

`formatDate` renders a calendar day and discards the time of day, so a Relative_Time computed from the underlying instant would describe information the cell does not show. An anchor has to be chosen because the two available choices give visibly different phrases: a value rendered `2026-03-12` whose instant is 00:58 in-zone is either "about 3 hours away" or "tomorrow", depending on which anchor is used, and the user can only check the phrase against the day they can see.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. WHERE a Date_Render_Position renders its value through `formatDate` — the date-only form — THE Formatted_Date component SHALL compute the Relative_Time from the Midnight_Anchor: 00:00 in the Resolved_Display_Timezone on the calendar day the cell displays.
2. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL anchor `now` the same way for those values — 00:00 in the Resolved_Display_Timezone on today's calendar day — so the distance is a whole number of days and the phrase is a whole number of day-or-larger units. Anchoring only one end would reintroduce exactly the hidden time-of-day this requirement removes.
3. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL NOT compute a date-only value's Relative_Time from the underlying instant's time of day. Two cells showing the same calendar day would otherwise carry different phrases, which reads as a defect rather than as precision.
4. WHERE a date-only value's Midnight_Anchor equals today's, THE Relative_Time SHALL be `just now`, that distance being zero and therefore in the first Relative_Time_Bucket. A dedicated `today` / `yesterday` / `tomorrow` vocabulary was considered and rejected: it would be a second set of phrases outside the Relative_Time_Ladder, applying to some Date_Render_Positions and not others, and Requirement 1's ladder is the one vocabulary.
5. WHERE a Date_Render_Position renders its value through `formatDateTime` — the form that shows `HH:MM` — THE Formatted_Date component SHALL compute the Relative_Time from the value's own instant and an unanchored `now`, since the time of day is visible in the cell and the finer buckets of the Relative_Time_Ladder are meaningful for it.
6. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL derive the Midnight_Anchor in the Resolved_Display_Timezone and SHALL NOT derive it in the browser's local zone. Using local midnight would reproduce the original defect device-management Requirement 18 was written to fix: a UTC−7 browser rendered `2026-03-11` for an instant of `2026-03-12T00:58:04.508Z`, a calendar day away from the operator's own zone.
7. THE Formatted_Date component SHALL retain the existing Imminent_Expiry and Expired_Certificate_State text markers on the device Expires cell unchanged (device-management Criteria 21.3, 21.5). **The overlap is accepted:** a Date_Tooltip reading `in 12 days` partly restates an `Expires soon` marker beside it. The markers stay because they are visible without a hover and are what carry the state to a screen reader, which a tooltip on an adjacent value is not; and the tooltip stays because suppressing it on one cell would make that cell the one place in the application where hovering a date does nothing.
8. THE Midnight_Anchor derivation SHALL be covered by unit tests asserting a value whose in-zone time of day falls near either end of a day, and a value whose local-zone and Resolved_Display_Timezone calendar days differ, so that Criterion 4.6 cannot regress to a local-zone anchor undetected.

### Requirement 5: The Channel-Tree Folder Icon Meets the Graphical-Object Contrast Minimum

**User Story:** As a user in dark mode, I want the folder icons in the channel tree to be visible, so that I can see the tree structure I am navigating.

Measured: `text-blue-600` (#2563eb) on `bg-gray-600` (#4b5563) is **1.46:1**, against the Graphical_Object_Minimum of 3:1. See the Measurement corrections in the introduction — the reported ~1.9:1 belongs to the Expandable_Channel_Row, and the background change alone does not clear the threshold.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Folder_Icon SHALL meet or exceed the Graphical_Object_Minimum of 3:1 against the Channel_Tree_Row background it sits on, in dark mode and in light mode, and in both the resting and the hover state of that row.
2. THE Channel_Tree_Pages SHALL both be changed together and identically. The Folder_Row markup is duplicated verbatim in `client/src/pages/Dashboard.jsx` (~327–330) and `client/src/pages/GlobalChannels.jsx` (~211–214); changing one and not the other leaves a measured contrast failure live on one of the two pages and the pages visually divergent.
3. THE Folder_Row dark background SHALL become `dark:bg-gray-800` (#1f2937), replacing `dark:bg-gray-600`.
4. THE Folder_Icon SHALL additionally become `dark:text-blue-400` (#60a5fa), replacing the single unconditional `text-blue-600`. **This change is load-bearing, not headroom, and the reported reasoning for it is corrected here.** `text-blue-600` on `bg-gray-800` is **2.84:1** — still BELOW the 3:1 minimum — so Criterion 5.3 alone does not achieve Criterion 5.1. With `dark:text-blue-400` the ratio is **5.77:1**. (The 3.4:1 that was reported for the background-only change is `text-blue-600` on `gray-900`, a darker row than Criterion 5.3 specifies. Moving the background to `gray-900` instead was considered and rejected: 3.43:1 sits within rounding distance of the threshold, and `gray-900` is the application's page background, so a row painted with it would stop reading as a row.)
5. THE Folder_Icon SHALL keep `text-blue-600` in light mode, where it measures **4.70:1** on `bg-gray-100` resting and **4.17:1** on `bg-gray-200` hover. Both already clear the Graphical_Object_Minimum, and THE Channel_Tree_Pages SHALL NOT regress either figure while fixing dark mode. The light-mode combination SHALL be asserted alongside the dark-mode one under Requirement 7, so "we only changed dark mode" is a checked claim rather than an assumption.
6. THE Channel_Tree_Pages SHALL convey the folder's expanded and collapsed states by the existing glyph swap (`FolderIcon` / `FolderOpenIcon`) and the Disclosure_Chevron's rotation, and SHALL NOT introduce colour as a state signal in the course of this change, consistent with device-management Criterion 16.5.
7. THE Disclosure_Chevron SHALL also meet the Graphical_Object_Minimum against the resting and hover backgrounds of the row it sits in, becoming `dark:text-gray-300` (#d1d5db) or lighter. **This is an addition beyond what was reported and is flagged for review.** The measurements are the reason it is included rather than deferred: the chevron is the row's only expanded/collapsed affordance and therefore a user-interface component under SC 1.4.11, and today's `text-gray-500` (#6b7280) measures **2.13:1** on the Expandable_Channel_Row's `gray-700` and **1.56:1** on the Folder_Row's `gray-600` hover — both failures, in the same rows and for the same reason as the Folder_Icon. After Criteria 5.3 and 6.1 it would sit at 3.04:1 resting, which is a pass by 0.04. `dark:text-gray-300` measures **9.96:1** resting and **5.13:1** against the toggle button's `dark:hover:bg-gray-600`.
8. THE Channel_Tree_Pages SHALL leave the Folder_Icon's size and spacing classes (`h-5 w-5`, `mr-2`) and the glyph choice unchanged. This requirement changes colour values only.

### Requirement 6: The Two Channel-Tree Row Types Share One Background

**User Story:** As a user, I want the folder rows and the expandable-channel rows in the channel tree to look like one tree, so that the two row types do not drift apart visually.

The Folder_Row is `bg-gray-100 dark:bg-gray-600` and the adjacent Expandable_Channel_Row in `Dashboard.jsx` (~262) is `bg-gray-50 dark:bg-gray-700`. Two row types in one tree with two backgrounds and two dark-mode failure ratios is how the 1.46:1 and 1.99:1 measurements came to be different numbers in the first place.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Channel_Tree_Rows SHALL share one resting background pair — `bg-gray-100 dark:bg-gray-800`, the pair Criterion 5.3 establishes — so the Folder_Row and the Expandable_Channel_Row cannot diverge.
2. THE Channel_Tree_Rows SHALL keep their hover states distinguishable from their resting states after Criterion 6.1. A resting background moved from `gray-600` to `gray-800` leaves the Folder_Row's existing `dark:hover:bg-gray-500` a large jump away and, worse, reintroduces a contrast failure: `dark:text-blue-400` on `gray-500` measures **1.90:1**, far below the Graphical_Object_Minimum — leaving the hover as it is would make it the WORST-contrast state in the row, worse even than the 1.46:1 resting failure this spec exists to fix. The hover therefore SHALL change with the resting state rather than being left as it is.
3. THE Folder_Row dark hover background SHALL become `dark:hover:bg-gray-700` (#374151), on which `dark:text-blue-400` measures **4.05:1** and `dark:text-gray-300` **7.00:1**, both clear of the Graphical_Object_Minimum.
4. THE Folder_Row hover SHALL remain perceivable as a change of state: the `gray-800` → `gray-700` step is a background-to-background ratio of **1.42:1**, larger than the existing light-mode `gray-100` → `gray-200` hover step of **1.13:1** that this application already ships and users already read as a hover. A step invisible in dark mode would remove the row's affordance while fixing its icon.
5. THE Channel_Tree_Pages SHALL unify the BACKGROUND and hover colours only, and SHALL NOT change the Expandable_Channel_Row's `ml-6` indentation, its `justify-between` layout, its permission badges, or its toggle button. The indentation encodes tree depth; removing it in the name of unification would flatten the hierarchy the tree exists to show.
6. THE Channel_Tree_Rows SHALL keep their own text clear of the Text_Contrast_Minimum of 4.5:1 on the unified background: the row heading `text-gray-900 dark:text-gray-100` measures **13.34:1** on `gray-800` and the description `text-gray-500 dark:text-gray-400` measures **5.78:1**. Both SHALL be asserted under Requirement 7 rather than assumed, since a darker resting background changes every ratio in the row and not only the icon's.
7. THE Expandable_Channel_Row's permission badges SHALL be left unchanged. They are fixed light-palette pills (`bg-blue-100 text-blue-800`, `bg-purple-100 text-purple-800`) whose contrast does not depend on the row background at all, and altering them is outside both concerns in this document.

### Requirement 7: Contrast Ratios Are Computed and Asserted, Not Eyeballed

**User Story:** As a maintainer, I want the contrast figures in this spec checked by a test, so that a later palette change cannot silently reintroduce the failure this spec exists to fix.

#### Acceptance Criteria

1. THE Channel_Tree_Pages work SHALL be covered by a test that COMPUTES each Contrast_Ratio from the sRGB colour values using the WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance formula, and SHALL NOT assert only against hardcoded expected ratio numbers. A hardcoded expectation records what someone believed at the time; a computed one records what the colours actually do.
2. THE contrast test SHALL derive the colour tokens it measures from the class names the Channel_Tree_Pages actually render — rendering the component and reading the resting and hover background classes and the icon colour classes off the produced elements — and SHALL NOT be fed a hand-written list of tokens. A test given its own copy of the tokens passes unchanged after someone edits the JSX, which is the one failure this requirement exists to prevent.
3. THE contrast test SHALL resolve each Tailwind token to its hex value through the Color_Token_Table, held in ONE place and covering only the tokens the Channel_Tree_Rows use. THE Color_Token_Table SHALL derive its values from `client/tailwind.config.js` and SHALL NOT hardcode hex literals or assume the stock Tailwind palette, so that a palette edit in the config flows through into these contrast assertions. This project DECLARES its own `gray` scale (50 through 900) under `theme.extend.colors.gray` in that config, alongside a custom `primary` scale; the declared scale is currently byte-identical to stock Tailwind's `gray`, and **that coincidence is precisely why the indirection is required rather than optional** — a table of stock Tailwind hex values would pass today and silently stop measuring the real colours the moment someone edits the config. IF a rendered class name resolves to no entry in the Color_Token_Table, THEN THE contrast test SHALL fail rather than skip that pair, so a token changed to one the table does not know about is reported instead of silently unmeasured.
4. THE contrast test SHALL assert each measured pair against its threshold as an inequality — `>= 3` for a Folder_Icon or Disclosure_Chevron pair against the Graphical_Object_Minimum, `>= 4.5` for a row-text pair against the Text_Contrast_Minimum — so that any change improving a ratio passes and only a regression below the threshold fails.
5. THE contrast test SHALL cover all of: the Folder_Icon and the Disclosure_Chevron against the resting and hover backgrounds in BOTH dark and light mode (Criteria 5.1, 5.5, 5.7), the row heading and description text against the unified background (Criterion 6.6), and the resting-to-hover background step of Criterion 6.4.
6. THE contrast test SHALL cover both Channel_Tree_Pages, not one of them, so the duplication Criterion 5.2 has to keep in step is checked on both sides rather than on whichever page the test happened to import.
7. THE contrast test SHALL fail on the pre-change markup. A test that passes before the fix is applied is not measuring the fix; the current 1.46:1 Folder_Icon pair is the assertion that must be red first.
8. THE Channel_Tree_Pages work SHALL NOT be verified by visual inspection or screenshot comparison alone. This requirement exists because the failure it fixes shipped through exactly that: the icon looked dim rather than looking like a conformance failure, and a number was needed before anyone could say which.
